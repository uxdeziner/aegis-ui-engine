Before anything goes public

Two distinctions to keep straight in every piece of content:

  1. Automated contrast checks are not accessibility certification. Say "contrast-checked against WCAG AA/AAA". Never say "WCAG compliant", "accessible by default" or "certified".
  2. Figma mock tests are not real Figma validation. Say "tested against a mock of the Figma API" until the plugin has been run in the Figma desktop app.

Claims checklist

  • No benchmark, speed-up, cost-saving or ROI numbers unless they come from a measured pilot, with the method stated.
  • Counts match the repo: 12 presets, 16 text styles, 13 component recipes, 6 Figma variable collections, 4 CLI output files, about 39 contrast pairs per mode.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts. Mention --no-fonts if someone asks about CSP or self-hosting.
  • Screenshots come from the current build, and fictional product names in previews are presented as sample data.

The full drafts, the demo shot list and the complete checklist are in launch-kit/LAUNCH-GUIDE.md.
